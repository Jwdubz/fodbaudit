What your site is costing you — Factory of Dreams

I'm Jarrett, a web designer here in Las Vegas. I went through your site the way a family planning a quinceañera or wedding would, on a phone. Here's what I saw.

The short version: your site gives people real ways to say yes. There's a "Book Now" page with an actual calendar, a tour page, a contact form and a tappable phone number. The trouble is weight and readability on a phone.

1. The homepage is heavy and sluggish on phones. My phone test pulled down about 11 MB just for the homepage. The main image took 5.8 seconds to show, and the page didn't respond to taps for over a second while it loaded. Families planning a quinceañera or a wedding are often browsing on their phones. A slow first page is where many of them give up.

2. The booking calendar is squeezed into a small box. On a phone, the "Book Now" page shows the appointment picker in a short window inside the page. It's easy to miss that there's more to scroll, so some people won't get past the first option.

3. A lot of your text is hard to read. My phone test flagged 15 spots, mostly small grey-on-black headings, that are too faint against the dark background. Ten of the gallery controls also aren't labelled for screen readers, and neither are your Facebook and Instagram icons.

What's working: two venues explained clearly, a package page for every type of event, real online booking, a tour page with both virtual and in-person options, and a contact form with spam protection.

What we couldn't measure: how many people visit, where you rank, and whether booking requests and contact messages reach you. I didn't submit anything, so all of that is unmeasured.
