I went through your site the way a family planning an event would, on a phone. Here's what I saw.

The short version

Your site gives people real ways to say yes. There's a “Book Now” page with an actual calendar, a tour page, a contact form and a tappable phone number. The trouble is weight and readability on a phone.

What I found

01Costing you most

The homepage is heavy and sluggish on phones.

What I saw

My phone test pulled down about 11 MB just for the homepage. The main image took 5.8 seconds to show, and the page didn't respond to taps for over a second while it loaded.

Why it matters

Families planning a quinceañera or a wedding are often browsing on their phones. A slow first page is where many of them give up.

The fix

Compress and resize the homepage images and video, and delay anything below the first screen until it's needed.

02Costing you most

The booking calendar is squeezed into a small box.

What I saw

On a phone, the “Book Now” page shows the appointment picker in a short window inside the page.

Why it matters

It's easy to miss that there's more to scroll, so some people won't get past the first option.

The fix

Let the booking picker use the full height of the phone screen so every option is visible as people scroll.

03Worth fixing

A lot of your text is hard to read.

What I saw

My phone test flagged 15 spots, mostly small grey-on-black headings, that are too faint against the dark background. Ten of the gallery controls also aren't labelled for screen readers, and neither are your Facebook and Instagram icons.

The fix

Brighten those headings, and add short labels to the gallery controls and social icons.

What's working

Two venues explained clearly, a package page for every type of event, real online booking, a tour page with both virtual and in-person options, and a contact form with spam protection.

What I couldn't measure

How many people visit, where you rank, and whether booking requests and contact messages reach you. I didn't submit anything, so all of that is unmeasured.
